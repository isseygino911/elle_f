# Arco — Student Manual

*Role: **Student**. You're here to learn: watch, practise, hand work in, and
book lessons.*

> 中文版：[zh/04-student.md](zh/04-student.md)

Everything you see in Arco is your own. You cannot see other students, and they
cannot see you — even if you share a teacher.

---

## 1. Getting in

Your teacher or the studio owner sends you an invitation link. Open it, choose a
name, email and password, and press *Create account*.

![Accepting an invitation](screenshots/shared-01-accept-invite-register.jpg)

Then log in — signing up does not log you in automatically.

---

## 2. Your dashboard

![Student dashboard](screenshots/student-01-dashboard.jpg)

Three counters across the top: **Homework due**, **Sessions next 24h**, and
**Unread messages**. The homework counter tells you the window too — *"due · 14
days"*.

Your **Next session** sits in the green band, with the date and time shown in
**two timezones** so there's no confusion if you and your teacher are in
different countries. There's a **Cancel** button right there if you can't make it.

Below that: **Homework due**, **Upcoming bookings**, **Tasks**, and **Awaiting
review** (practice videos your teacher hasn't commented on yet).

> The **Upcoming bookings** panel lists your *later* lessons. Your very next one
> is already the green band at the top, so with only one lesson booked this
> panel correctly reads "No upcoming bookings". It isn't a mistake.

Your sidebar has six sections.

---

## 3. Your courses and homework

**Sidebar → Courses (02).** The courses you are enrolled in. Your teacher enrols
you — you can't join one yourself.

![Your courses](screenshots/student-02-course-curriculum.jpg)

Open a course to see its **Curriculum** — the homework in order, numbered, each
with its due date.

> You only see homework your teacher has **published**. Anything still being
> drafted is invisible to you until they release it.

### Handing work in

Click a piece of homework.

![A piece of homework](screenshots/student-03-homework-detail.jpg)

At the top: the instructions, an **Open reference** link if your teacher
attached one (a recording, a score, a page to work from), the due date, and how
many attempts you're allowed.

Under **Your work**, you'll see only the ways your teacher chose to accept:

- **Your answer** — type your response.
- **Attach files** — a scan, a photo, a document, an audio or video file. You
  can attach more than one.
- **Record a take** — record straight from your camera in the browser, if your
  teacher enabled it.

Fill in what applies and press **Hand in**. You can use several at once — they
go together as one submission.

![Handed in](screenshots/student-04-submitted.jpg)

Afterwards you'll see **Attempt 1 · Submitted** with the date. Two useful things
then happen:

- You can **edit your answer** and press *Save changes* — handy for a typo or an
  afterthought.
- If you have attempts left, a fresh form appears below for another go. The
  header tells you the limit (*"Attempts allowed: 3"*).

Your teacher's feedback appears on this same page once they've reviewed it.

---

## 4. Practice videos

**Sidebar → Videos (04).** The videos you've sent your teacher, and lesson
videos they've shared with you. *Awaiting review* on your dashboard counts the
ones your teacher hasn't commented on yet.

To add one, press **+ Upload video**:

![Uploading a practice video](screenshots/student-06-video-upload.jpg)

- **Record with your camera** — records in the browser; it will ask permission.
- **Or choose a video file** — MP4, WebM or QuickTime, up to **2 GiB**.
- Give it a **title**.

Your video is always filed as **practice, uploaded as yourself** — there's
nothing to choose. Only you and your teacher can see it.

---

## 5. Lessons

**Sidebar → Bookings (05).** Your lessons, and your teacher's free slots.

![Your bookings](screenshots/student-01-dashboard.jpg)

- **Your upcoming bookings** on the left, each marked *Scheduled*, with **✕** to
  cancel.
- **Available slots** on the right. Move between days with **Previous day** and
  **Next day**, then click a time to book it.

Times always appear in **both timezones** (for example *8:00 AM ET / 8:00 PM
China*) — read whichever is yours.

When a lesson is close enough to start, a **Join** link appears. The lesson
happens as a video call in your browser; there's nothing to install.

You don't set availability — those slots come from your teacher's calendar.

---

## 6. Messages

**Sidebar → Messages (06).** One private conversation with your studio. It opens
straight into the thread — there's no list to pick from, because you have only
one.

![Your messages](screenshots/student-05-messages.jpg)

Each message is labelled with who sent it. Both your **teacher** and the
**studio owner** can write here, and both can read your replies — so treat it as
talking to the studio rather than to one person. Type at the bottom and press
**Send**.

Managers can never read these messages.

---

## 7. Library

**Sidebar → Library (03).** Shared handouts, sheet music and files the studio has
published for everyone.

![The library](screenshots/student-07-library-readonly.jpg)

Browse by category or use the search box. You can read and download anything
here, but only teachers and owners can add files.

---

## 8. Everyday bits

- **Notifications** — the bell above your name: new homework, feedback,
  announcements. **Mark read**, or **Mark all read**.
- **Language** — click your name at the bottom of the sidebar: **English** or
  **中文**.
- **Log out** — same menu.

---

## What's private, and what isn't

Worth knowing exactly where you stand:

| Thing | Who can see it |
|---|---|
| Your homework submissions | You and your teacher |
| Your practice videos | You and your teacher |
| Your messages | You, your teacher, and the studio owner |
| Your bookings | You and your teacher |
| **Other students** | **Not visible to you — and you're not visible to them** |

Even a student who shares your teacher cannot see your work, your videos, your
messages, or that you exist. Managers see totals for the studio, never your
work.

---

## Your typical week

1. Check the dashboard — homework due, next lesson.
2. **Courses** → open this week's homework, read the instructions.
3. Practise, then **Hand in** — written notes, a file, or a recorded take.
4. **Videos** → send a practice video if your teacher asked for one.
5. **Bookings** → book next week's lesson from your teacher's free slots.
6. **Messages** → ask anything you got stuck on.
